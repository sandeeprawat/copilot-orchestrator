# Satya Nadella's 10 Most Notable LinkedIn Posts (2024–2026)

> **Methodology note:** LinkedIn does not publicly expose per-post engagement rankings. This list was compiled by cross-referencing news coverage, third-party analytics (LinkHub, Favikon), and media reports of posts that generated the most external discussion, shares, and coverage. Nadella averages ~5,100 likes per post with a 2.08% engagement rate and posts roughly twice per week to his 11.4M+ followers ([LinkHub](https://linkhub.gg/en/top-creators-2025/creator/ACoAAAEkwwAB9KEc2TrQgOLEQ-vzRyZeCDyc6DQ), [Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-most-followed-linkedin-influencers)).

## 1. 🎬 "Less Hair. Same Love for Excel!" — The Viral 1993 Demo Reaction
**Timeframe:** Late 2025  
**What happened:** A 1993 video of a young Nadella enthusiastically demoing Microsoft Excel resurfaced on X/Twitter. Nadella responded on social media with a side-by-side photo — the old clip next to a recent image of himself wearing a hoodie reading *"Making Sheet Happen Since 1985"* — captioned: *"Less hair. Same love for Excel!"*  
**Why it went viral:** The self-deprecating humor, nostalgia, and authenticity drove millions of views and massive engagement. It became one of his most shared moments across platforms, with other tech CEOs jumping into the thread.  
**Sources:** [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/less-hair-same-love-for-excel-microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-s-32-year-old-clip-breaks-the-internet-article-13687522.html) · [MSN/Hindustan Times](https://www.msn.com/en-in/technology/tech-companies/less-hair-same-love-satya-nadella-reacts-to-his-32-year-old-microsoft-excel-demo-going-viral/ar-AA1QPLK4) · [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/can-get-your-hair-back-california-company-ceos-message-for-satya-nadella-goes-viral-3805993)

## 2. 📝 "Looking Ahead to 2026" — The AI Reset Blog Post
**Date:** December 29, 2025  
**Content:** Nadella launched a personal blog ("sn scratchpad" on GitHub Pages) and shared its inaugural essay on LinkedIn. He urged the industry to move past the "AI slop vs. sophistication" debate and focus on AI as *"scaffolding for human potential."* Key themes: moving from models to systems, deliberate AI diffusion, and AI as a "cognitive amplifier" echoing Steve Jobs' "bicycles for the mind" metaphor.  
**Impact:** Covered by TechCrunch, TechRadar, Bloomberg, Business Today, Economic Times, and Fast Company. Sparked the "Microslop" counter-meme on social media.  
**Sources:** [snscratchpad.com (original)](https://snscratchpad.com/posts/looking-ahead-2026/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/05/microsofts-nadella-wants-us-to-stop-thinking-of-ai-as-slop/) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/pro/satya-nadella-wants-us-to-stop-calling-ai-slop-good-luck-with-that) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-01-08/microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-shows-new-leadership-persona-in-blog) · [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/new-updates/microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-calls-for-a-big-ai-reset-in-2026-says-we-need-to-move-beyond-/articleshow/126331455.cms)

**Timeframe:** September 2025  
**Content:** Nadella shared five specific Copilot prompts he uses every day — for meeting preparation, project-risk assessment, launch tracking, time-allocation analysis, and email-driven meeting prep. This offered a rare, tactical look at how a Fortune 10 CEO leverages generative AI in daily workflows.  
**Impact:** Widely covered across tech and mainstream press; praised by professionals adapting to AI-powered workplaces. The actionable, "how I use it" format drove high engagement.

## 4. 💼 "Recommitting to Our Why, What, and How" — Open Letter on Layoffs
**Date:** July 2025  
**Content:** Following Microsoft layoffs despite record financial performance, Nadella published an internal memo (widely shared and referenced on LinkedIn) addressing the "enigma of success" — why a company performing at its peak still makes painful cuts. He discussed the need for continuous "unlearning and learning," reaffirmed Microsoft's mission, and addressed the emotional toll on employees.  
**Impact:** Resonated both internally and externally; extensively covered by news outlets. The transparency and candor broke from typical corporate-speak.  
**Sources:** [Microsoft Official Blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2025/07/24/recommitting-to-our-why-what-and-how/) · [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/world-news/microsoft-layoffs-ceo-memo-explains-reason-to-employees-enigma-of-success-101753438801189.html)

## 5. 🌐 Microsoft Build 2025 — "The Era of the Open Agentic Web"
**Date:** May 2025  
**Content:** Nadella shared Build 2025 announcements on LinkedIn, declaring the start of the *"Open Agentic Web"* — an internet where autonomous AI agents interact, make decisions, and complete workflows across platforms. Highlights included the GitHub Copilot coding agent, Azure AI Foundry with 1,900+ models, NLWeb/MCP protocols for agent interoperability, and Copilot Tuning for enterprises.  
**Impact:** The "agentic AI" framing became the dominant narrative in tech media for weeks.  
**Sources:** [Microsoft Build 2025](https://news.microsoft.com/build-2025/) · [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/deeptech/tech/microsoft-build-2025-live-ceo-satya-nadella-to-unveil-copilot-ai-enhancements-and-azure-innovations) · [CRN](https://www.crn.com/news/ai/2025/microsoft-build-2025-ceo-nadella-takes-platform-systems-approach-to-the-agentic-web) · [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/technology/microsoft-builf-2025-satya-nadella-artificial-intelligence-agents-copilot-ws-l-19606923.htm)

## 6. 🇮🇳 AI's Impact on India's 300M Informal Workers
**Timeframe:** December 2025  
**Content:** Nadella posted about Microsoft's partnership with India's Ministry of Labour to connect over 300 million informal workers to jobs, welfare, and social security via AI-powered platforms (e-Shram, NCS portal on Azure). He framed it as a model for inclusive growth — technology serving marginalized communities, not just enterprises. Part of Microsoft's $17.5B India investment.  
**Impact:** Widely praised in Indian and international business media; exemplified Nadella's recurring theme of AI for societal good.  
**Sources:** [EnterpriseZone](https://enterprisezone.cc/satya-nadella-highlights-ais-impact-on-indias-informal-workforce-via-linkedin-post/) · [Microsoft News](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/2025/12/09/microsoft-invests-us17-5-billion-in-india-to-drive-ai-diffusion-at-population-scale/) · [AsiaNet News](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/business/satya-nadella-on-ais-profound-impact-on-300m-indian-informal-workers-articleshow-f1zkuvv) · [Entrepreneur India](https://www.entrepreneur.com/en-in/news-and-trends/the-ai-era-why-satya-nadella-is-tapping-on-indias-talent/500690)

## 7. 🎤 Microsoft Ignite 2024 — Copilot Agents & the "Agentic World"
**Date:** November 2024  
**Content:** Nadella shared his Ignite 2024 keynote highlights, unveiling 5 new Copilot agents (Facilitator for Teams, Project Manager for Planner, SharePoint agent, Interpreter for Teams, and Copilot Actions for workflow automation). He described a multimodal, memory-equipped "agentic world" and noted ~70% of Fortune 500 were already using Copilot.  
**Impact:** Defined the "agentic AI" vocabulary that dominated 2025 tech discourse.  
**Sources:** [Observer](https://observer.com/2024/11/microsoft-satya-nadella-agentic-ai/) · [Hable](https://www.hable.co.uk/news/microsoft-ignite-announcements-2024) · [Launch Consulting](https://www.launchconsulting.com/posts/5-key-takeaways-from-satya-nadella-at-microsoft-ignite-2024) · [Microsoft 365 Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2024/11/19/introducing-copilot-actions-new-agents-and-tools-to-empower-it-teams/)

## 8. 👥 India AI Impact Summit 2026 — Tech Leaders Stage Moment
**Date:** March 2026  
**Content:** Nadella participated in India's AI Impact Summit alongside Sam Altman and Sundar Pichai. The moment went viral as a meme dubbed the trio *"Satyam Shivam Sundaram"* (a play on the Sanskrit phrase meaning "Truth, Goodness, Beauty"). While the meme itself was user-generated, Nadella's related posts about safe/trusted AI and India's AI potential amplified the cultural moment.  
**Note:** The "Satyam Shivam Sundaram" meme originated from social media users, not from Nadella's own post. His LinkedIn activity around the summit focused on responsible AI and India's role.  
**Sources:** [Times Now](https://www.timesnownews.com/viral/viral-post-alert-microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-openai-ceo-sam-altman-google-ceo-sundar-pichai-the-new-satyam-shivam-sundaram-of-artificial-intelligence-india-ai-impact-summit-2026-article-153621056)
