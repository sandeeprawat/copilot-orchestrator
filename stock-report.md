## 7. Risk Factors & What Could Go Wrong

1. **Oil Shock Scenario:** A sustained rise in crude above $90/barrel would hurt India's current account deficit, weaken INR, and pressure inflation — potentially reversing RBI's dovish stance.

2. **FII Selling Acceleration:** If US rates stay elevated or dollar strengthens, FII outflows could intensify, creating downward pressure especially on large-caps.

3. **Earnings Miss:** If Q4 FY26 earnings disappoint (especially in IT, Banking), the market could see a sharp correction from current levels.

4. **Geopolitical Escalation:** US-Iran tensions, China-Taiwan situation, or regional instability could trigger risk-off sentiment globally.

5. **Valuation Correction:** At ~20x forward PE, Nifty is priced for perfection. Any negative surprise could trigger a 10-15% correction.
