## 1. Helios: Real-Time Long Video Generation Model
**Source:** ByteDance · [Hugging Face Trending](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending)

A **14-billion-parameter** autoregressive diffusion model capable of efficient, high-quality, real-time long-video synthesis. Helios represents a major leap toward scalable long-form video content generation without requiring conventional optimization tricks. Demos are available on Hugging Face Spaces.

> **Why it matters:** Bridges the gap between short video clips and full-length content generation, with direct applications in media, entertainment, and synthetic data creation.

---

## 2. DeepPlanning: Benchmarking Long-Horizon Agentic Planning
**Source:** arXiv · [Hugging Face Trending](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending)

Introduces a significantly more complex and realistic **planning benchmark for LLMs** featuring verifiable constraints and real-world multi-step tasks. Tests how well agentic models handle long-horizon decision-making under uncertainty.

> **Why it matters:** As AI agents take on more autonomous roles (coding, research, operations), rigorous planning benchmarks are essential to measure and improve their reliability.

---

## 3. DeepRare: AI System for Rare Disease Diagnosis
**Source:** Shanghai Jiao Tong University · [HumAI Blog](https://www.humai.blog/ai-news-trends-march-2026-complete-monthly-digest/)

An integrated diagnostic system that combines **40+ specialized AI tools** to diagnose rare diseases, outperforming human doctors in several clinical benchmarks. Uses a multi-expert architecture where different models collaborate on differential diagnosis.

> **Why it matters:** Rare diseases affect ~400 million people globally but suffer from delayed diagnosis. DeepRare demonstrates how orchestrated AI systems can meaningfully improve patient outcomes.

---

## 4. EmCoop: A Framework and Benchmark for Embodied Cooperation Among LLM Agents
**Source:** arXiv:2603.00349 · [arXiv cs.AI](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current)

Proposes a new benchmark for simulating **embodied cooperation** between multiple LLM-powered agents — agents that must physically and cognitively collaborate in shared environments. Covers task decomposition, communication protocols, and coordination strategies.

> **Why it matters:** Pushes AI beyond single-agent systems toward teams of agents that can cooperate in robotics, logistics, and real-world physical tasks.

---

## 5. AlphaEvolve: Autonomous Evolutionary Coding Agent
**Source:** Google DeepMind (Gemini-based) · [OutrightCRM Research](https://www.outrightcrm.com/blog/research-papers-on-ai/)

An agent built on **Gemini technology** that autonomously creates, tests, and refines algorithms through evolutionary coding. It generates candidate solutions, evaluates them, and iteratively improves — effectively self-improving its own code.

> **Why it matters:** Signals a shift toward AI systems that can autonomously discover and optimize algorithms, reducing the need for human-directed engineering in algorithm design.
